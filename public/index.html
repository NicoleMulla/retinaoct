About. This atlas indexes the OLIVES dataset — OCT B-scans from the PRIME and TREX-DME clinical trials, paired with expert biomarker annotations, best-corrected visual acuity (BCVA) and central subfield thickness (CST). Research use only; not a medical device and not for diagnosis. No patient-identifiable data is published.

Data source. OLIVES, Georgia Tech OLIVES Lab. Images are de-identified and published for research.
