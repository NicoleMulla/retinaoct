Data sources

Every image in this atlas comes from openly published research data. Sources, licences and required citations are listed in full below.

Currently indexed

OLIVES — Ophthalmic Labels for Investigating Visual Eye Semantics

OCT B-scans from two diabetic eye disease clinical trials, paired with expert biomarker annotations and per-visit clinical measurements. This is the only dataset currently searchable in the atlas.

FieldValue
Images indexed78,283
With expert biomarkers9,408
Patients / eyes87 / 96
TrialsTREX-DME (45,848) · PRIME (32,435)
Visits48 distinct timepoints
LicenceCC BY 4.0

Required citation. Prabhushankar, M., Kokilepersaud, K., Logan, Y., Trejo Corona, S., AlRegib, G., and Wykoff, C., “OLIVES Dataset: Ophthalmic Labels for Investigating Visual Eye Semantics”, NeurIPS Datasets and Benchmarks Track, 2022. doi:10.5281/zenodo.6622145

Images displayed here are downsampled WebP derivatives. The source data is unmodified and available from the link above.
