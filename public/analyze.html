Analyze a scan

Upload an OCT B-scan and our model predicts which of sixteen retinal biomarkers are present. The model is a RETFound ViT-L/16 encoder fine-tuned on 9,408 expert-annotated OLIVES B-scans.

Research use only. This is not a medical device and must not be used for diagnosis. The model was validated on Heidelberg Spectralis scans from 87 patients in two diabetic macular oedema trials. Anything outside that distribution is flagged, not silently guessed at.

Drop an OCT image here or click to choose a file PNG, JPEG, TIFF, BMP or WebP · up to 8 MB

Your image is sent to the model, analyzed, and discarded. It is never stored, logged or added to the atlas.
